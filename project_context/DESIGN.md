# Design

## North Star

An 8-bit arcade game that brings the charm and immediacy of 1980s arcade classics to a business simulation. Players should feel the same mix of tension and control that made games like Tapper compelling — fast decisions, clear feedback, satisfying progression.

## Design Principles

**Single-screen arcade design**
- All action takes place on one landscape screen, like classic arcade games
- No scrolling, no scene transitions during gameplay
- Player sees everything that matters at once

**8-bit aesthetic purity**
- Pixelated graphics and monospace fonts
- CRT scanline effects
- Bright, high-contrast arcade color palette (greens, magentas, cyans, yellows)
- Sharp borders, no rounded corners
- Glow effects for emphasis

**Desktop-first, touch-capable**
- Primary input: mouse
- Secondary input: touchscreen (phones/tablets in landscape)
- All interactions should work with both input methods

**Tapper-inspired immediacy**
- Fast-paced decision making
- Clear cause and effect
- Visual feedback for every action
- Escalating challenge
